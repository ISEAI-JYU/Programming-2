Choose a project topic that interests you.

You may choose one of the predefined topics from the project guidelines page or create a project based on your own idea.

**If you choose a predefined topic**, submit the name of the topic you selected:
(Expense Tracking,
Product Inventory Management,
Library,
Housing Company Management,
Flashcard Application)

**If you create a project based on your own idea**, write and submit a project plan.

The plan must include:

- The purpose of the application.
- The functional requirements that are essential for the application.
- A description of the entities that are important to model within the application's domain.

You may use the descriptions of the predefined topics as examples.
After submitting, present the plan to an instructor in person or through remote supervision before starting the remaining project phases.

**For pair projects**, the application must contain at least three modeled entities (individual projects require two; requirement 1.1) and three views (individual projects require two; requirement 4.1). For this reason, pair project plans must always be reviewed by an instructor before proceeding to the next phases.
