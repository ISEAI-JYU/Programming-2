Write a short, individual reflection (about 300–600 words) on your project work. Each member of the pair writes their own reflection. Address the following:

- What did you learn during the project that you did not expect to?
- What was the hardest part, and how did you get past it (or why did you not)?
- How did you divide the work in your pair, how did the communication go, and what would you change as a pair?
- What would you do differently if you started the project again?
- Name one concrete thing you want to get better at in your next programming course.

The reflection is assessed on a pass/fail basis. It is accepted when it honestly addresses the questions above.
