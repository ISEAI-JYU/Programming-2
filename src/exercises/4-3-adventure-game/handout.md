Let's implement a simple text adventure game (or, more accurately, a small part of one), where the player can try to pick up items from the ground and eat food to gain energy.
You are provided with two interfaces (on the exercise page): `Edible` with the method `int eat()`, which eats the item and returns the amount of energy gained, and `Pickable` with the method `boolean tryPickUp(double freeBackpackSpace)`, which returns whether the item fits in the backpack (an item fits if its weight is at most the free space).
You are also provided with partially implemented classes: `Apple` (weight 0.2 kg, implements both interfaces) and `LargeRock` (weight 50.0 kg, implements `Pickable`). Eating an apple gives a positive amount of energy; you may choose the amount. The classes may also print messages of their own.
In addition, you are given a partially implemented main program (`Game.java`) that keeps track of the player's energy (50 at the start) and backpack capacity (10 kg).
