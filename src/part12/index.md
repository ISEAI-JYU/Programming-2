# Project Assignment Submission

Present your project assignment to an instructor or during an in-person guidance session by the announced deadline.

If the available guidance times do not suit you, contact the course staff and arrange a separate appointment.

Before the review, make sure that you have addressed all [project requirements](../project-assignment.md) and that the latest version of your project is available in your Git remote repository.

Be prepared to present your work and answer any questions the instructor may have about it.
